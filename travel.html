Travel

I’ve always loved traveling. As a kid, I loved Childcraft: The How and Why Library, especially the volume Places to Know. The place that stuck out to me most was Blarney Castle, which I ended up visiting years later.

In graduate school, my NSF GK-12 fellowship let me travel, and it opened up a whole new world for me. Since then, I’ve been to over 70 countries and hundreds of cities.
