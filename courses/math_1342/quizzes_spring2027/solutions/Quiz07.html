Problem 1 [2 pts]

For each design, decide whether you should use a paired or an independent two-sample approach, and explain why. (1 pt each)

  1. 25 students take a statistics pretest in January and the same test again in May.
  2. Commute times of 40 students at RRC are compared with commute times of 40 different students at Highland.

(a) Paired — each student is measured twice, so the two scores come in natural pairs. (b) Independent — the two samples are different people with no pairing.
