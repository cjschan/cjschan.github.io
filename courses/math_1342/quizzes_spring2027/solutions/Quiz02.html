Problem 1 [2 pts]

A survey asked 240 ACC students whether they attend full-time or part-time and whether they have a job.

Has a jobNo jobTotal
Full-time4872120
Part-time9030120
Total138102240
  1. What proportion of all surveyed students have a job? (1 pt)
  2. Compare the proportion of full-time students with a job to the proportion of part-time students with a job. (1 pt)

(a) \(138/240 = 0.575\).

(b) Full-time: \(48/120 = 0.40\); part-time: \(90/120 = 0.75\). Part-time students are much more likely to have a job (0.75 vs. 0.40).

Problem 2 [2 pts]

Find the mean and the median of \(3, 6, 6, 8, 10, 12, 15, 24\). Which one changes more if we replace 24 with 240? (1 mean, 0.5 median, 0.5 explain)

Mean \(= 84/8 = 10.5\). Median \(= (8+10)/2 = 9\).

With 240: mean \(= 300/8 = 37.5\); median stays 9. The mean is affected much more because it uses the value of every data point; the median is resistant to outliers.

Problem 4 [2 pts]

Scores on a placement test are roughly bell-shaped with mean 72 and standard deviation 6. (1 pt each)

  1. Use the 95% rule to give the interval that contains about 95% of scores.
  2. A student’s score is at the 90th percentile. Explain what this means in context.

(a) \(72 \pm 2(6) = (60, 84)\).

(b) About 90% of test takers scored at or below this student’s score (and about 10% scored higher).
