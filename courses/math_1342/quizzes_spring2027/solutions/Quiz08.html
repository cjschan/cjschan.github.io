Problem 1 [5 pts] STATKEY

In a random sample of n Austin adults, x said they ride public transit at least once a month.

Count xSample size nConfidence levelNull value \(p_0\)
13632595%0.35
  1. Compute \(\hat{p}\) and \(SE = \sqrt{\hat{p}(1-\hat{p})/n}\). (1 pt)
  2. Use Theoretical Distributions → Normal (mean 0, SD 1) with Two-Tail to find \(z^*\) for the confidence level. Screenshot it. (1 pt)
  3. Construct the confidence interval and interpret it in context. (1 pt)
  4. Test \(H_0: p = p_0\) vs. \(H_a: p \ne p_0\). Compute \(z = (\hat{p} - p_0)/\sqrt{p_0(1-p_0)/n}\) and use StatKey’s Normal distribution to find the p-value. Screenshot it. (1.5 pts)
  5. State your conclusion at \(\alpha = 0.05\). (0.5 pt)
\(\hat{p}\)\(z^*\)SECIzp-value
0.4181.9600.02740.365 to 0.4722.590.0097

Accept \(z^*\) and p-values to within rounding (\(\pm 0.01\) for \(z^*\), \(\pm 0.002\) for p-values). Interpretation: we are 95% confident that the proportion of all Austin adults who ride transit monthly is between the endpoints. The interval does not contain 0.35, and the p-value (0.0097) is below 0.05, so reject \(H_0\): the proportion of Austin adults who ride transit monthly differs from 0.35 (it is higher).
