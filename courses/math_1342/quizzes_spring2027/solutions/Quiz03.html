Problem 3 [2 pts]

True or false? Give a brief explanation for each. (1 pt each)

  1. Monthly ice cream sales and monthly drownings have \(r = 0.88\), so eating ice cream causes drowning.
  2. If every quiz score in Problem 2 were increased by 10 points, the correlation would increase.

(a) False. Correlation is not causation; hot weather (a confounding variable) increases both swimming and ice cream sales.

(b) False. Adding a constant shifts every point up the same amount; it does not change how tightly the points follow a line, so \(r\) is unchanged.
