StatKey problems. Problems marked STATKEY are done in StatKey (lock5stat.com/StatKey). For each StatKey problem, include a screenshot of your StatKey output with your answers.

Problem 1 [2 pts]

For each variable, decide whether it is categorical or quantitative. (0.5 pt each)

  1. Zip code where a student lives
  2. Commute time to campus (minutes)
  3. Blood type
  4. Number of credit hours a student is taking

Problem 2 [2 pts]

Match each scenario to the most appropriate sampling method (simple random, stratified, cluster, or convenience). (0.5 pt each)

  1. A reporter interviews the first 25 shoppers leaving a grocery store.
  2. A college numbers all 1,200 online students, and a computer picks 80 numbers at random.
  3. A district randomly selects 6 of its 40 schools and surveys every teacher at those 6 schools.
  4. A company splits employees into full-time and part-time groups and randomly selects 30 from each group.

Problem 3 [2 pts]

A researcher surveys 300 adults and finds that those who drink more coffee report sleeping fewer hours per night. (1 pt each)

  1. Is this an experiment or an observational study? Explain.
  2. Name a possible confounding variable and explain how it could create this association without coffee causing less sleep.
