  1. Find and use theoretical probabilities from normal, t, chi-squared and F distributions to form confidence intervals and test hypotheses. Apply the 95% rule to normal and to approximately normal distributions.
  2. Use the sampling distributions of sample proportions and sample means to answer appropriate questions.
  3. Estimate single means, difference of two means, single proportions and difference of two proportions using confidence intervals. Interpret the results.
  4. Demonstrate skills in hypothesis testing for means and proportions, for single populations and comparison of two populations.
  1. Explain why inference for a mean uses the t distribution with df = n − 1 instead of the normal distribution. (CO4)
  2. Check the conditions for t procedures (n ≥ 30, or roughly normal data without extreme outliers). (CO4, CO8)
  3. Construct and interpret a t confidence interval for a population mean, including the margin of error. (CO4, CO9)
  4. Carry out a one-sample t test for μ and state the conclusion in context. (CO4, CO10)
