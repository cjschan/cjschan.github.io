  1. Find and use empirical probabilities in bootstrap distributions to find confidence intervals and in randomization distributions to test hypotheses.
  2. Estimate single means, difference of two means, single proportions and difference of two proportions using confidence intervals. Interpret the results.
  3. Demonstrate skills in hypothesis testing for means and proportions, for single populations and comparison of two populations.
  1. Determine which percentiles of a bootstrap distribution bound a 90%, 95%, or 99% confidence interval, and construct the interval with StatKey. (CO3, CO9)
  2. Predict how changing the confidence level or the sample size affects the width of an interval. (CO9)
  3. Judge whether a bootstrap confidence interval is trustworthy from the shape of the bootstrap distribution and whether the sample is representative. (CO3)
  4. Identify the parameter of interest and write null and alternative hypotheses from a research question. (CO10)
  5. Classify a test as right-tailed, left-tailed, or two-tailed, and explain the logic of assuming H₀ and asking whether the data are surprising. (CO10)
