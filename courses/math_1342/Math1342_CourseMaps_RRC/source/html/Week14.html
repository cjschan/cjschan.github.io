  1. Find and use theoretical probabilities from normal, t, chi-squared and F distributions to form confidence intervals and test hypotheses. Apply the 95% rule to normal and to approximately normal distributions.
  2. Analyze data presented in two-way tables to provide information about relationships between categorical variables.
  3. Demonstrate skills in hypothesis testing using chi-squared tests.
  1. State the hypotheses for a chi-square test of association from a two-way table. (CO6, CO11)
  2. Compute expected counts and check that all expected counts are at least 5. (CO11)
  3. Compute the chi-square statistic and df = (r − 1)(c − 1), and interpret the right-tail p-value. (CO4, CO11)
  4. Relate the chi-square test on a 2×2 table to the two-proportion z test, and explain that a significant result shows association but not direction or causation. (CO11)
