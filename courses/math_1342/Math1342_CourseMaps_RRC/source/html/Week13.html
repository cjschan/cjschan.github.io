  1. Find and use theoretical probabilities from normal, t, chi-squared and F distributions to form confidence intervals and test hypotheses. Apply the 95% rule to normal and to approximately normal distributions.
  2. Apply ideas of appropriate sampling techniques and experimental design to data production.
  3. Use the sampling distributions of sample proportions and sample means to answer appropriate questions.
  4. Estimate single means, difference of two means, single proportions and difference of two proportions using confidence intervals. Interpret the results.
  5. Demonstrate skills in hypothesis testing for means and proportions, for single populations and comparison of two populations.
  1. Compute the standard error and conservative degrees of freedom for a difference in two independent means, and check the conditions. (CO4, CO8)
  2. Construct and interpret a confidence interval for μ₁ − μ₂, explaining how the order of the groups affects the sign. (CO9)
  3. Carry out a two-sample t test, choosing the tail from the research question. (CO10)
  4. Classify a study design as paired or independent samples by checking for a link between observations. (CO7, CO10)
  5. Define the difference variable d, carry out a paired t test and paired t confidence interval, and explain why pairing reduces variability. (CO9, CO10)
