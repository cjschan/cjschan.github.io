Course Level Objectives:

  • Explain the use of data collection and statistics as tools to reach reasonable conclusions. (CLO 1)
  • Recognize, examine, and interpret the basic principles of describing and presenting data. (CLO 2)
  • Explain the role of probability in statistics. (CLO 4)
Module ObjectivesInstructional MaterialsLearning ActivitiesAssessmentsTools

1. Describe statistics as the process of collecting, describing, and analyzing data. (CLO 1)

2. Determine whether a surprising result could reasonably have happened by chance alone. (CLO 4)

3. Distinguish between a sample and a population. (CLO 1)

4. Explain how a non-representative sample can bias an estimate. (CLO 1)

5. Compute and compare the mean and median of a small dataset, and explain how an outlier affects each. (CLO 2)
