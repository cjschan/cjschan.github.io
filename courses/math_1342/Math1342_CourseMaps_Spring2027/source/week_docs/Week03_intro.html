Introduction

Last week we looked at how data are sampled. This week we look at how they are produced. In 1.3 you will tell an experiment from an observational study and see why only randomized experiments can show cause and effect. In 2.1 we start describing data, beginning with categorical variables: proportions, two-way tables and bar charts. Watch out for the difference between random sampling (who is in the study) and random assignment (who gets which treatment).

This week covers: 1.3 Experiments and Observational Studies; 2.1 Categorical Variables.
