Practice and Class Activities

These are the activities we do in class this week. Participation items are completed in class.

  • Participation: Writing Hypotheses – rock paper scissors practice (9 of 11 wins): define the parameter, state H₀ and Hₐ, choose the tail
  • Your Turn: Write the Hypotheses (airline on-time rate, SAT prep, and more)
  • Think about it: is 15 of 16 correct (dolphin study) convincing?
  • StatKey – Randomization Hypothesis Tests
  • Participation: Randomization Distributions – StatKey: dolphin study (16 flips); count how many simulated statistics are as extreme as observed
  • Your Turn: Tea and Immunity in StatKey (re-randomize tea vs. coffee, find the p-value)
