Practice and Class Activities

These are the activities we do in class this week. Participation items are completed in class.

  • Participation: Inference for a Proportion – 4-step CI and 4-step test for a proportion (voting example); compare the two SEs
  • Your Turn: Testing a Proportion (60 of 250 live on campus)
  • Participation: Difference in Proportions – two-proportion randomization (18/30 vs. 12/30); compare simulation with the formula
  • Your Turn: Treatment vs Placebo (two-proportion z test from a two-way table)
