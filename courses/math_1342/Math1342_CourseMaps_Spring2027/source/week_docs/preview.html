Week 1 Introduction, Objectives, and Learning Guide

Introduction

Welcome to Elementary Statistics! This first class asks a simple question: what is statistics for? We will collect real data from our own class, summarize it with a mean, a median and a boxplot, and ask whether a surprising result could have happened just by chance. That question, “could it be chance?”, comes back all semester and is the heart of hypothesis testing in Weeks 9–15. Before class on Wednesday, take the Start Here tour and read the syllabus: the Syllabus Quiz, the first of your 9 quizzes, is taken in class that day.

This week covers: Introduction: What Is Statistics For? (Wed Jan 20; the semester begins Tue Jan 19).
