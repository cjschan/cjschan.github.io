Introduction

This week ties the course together. In 4.5 you will see how a confidence interval and a two-tailed test lead to the same decision, and when each fits a question. In 5.1 we replace the randomization distribution with the normal distribution, compute a z statistic and find p-values as areas under the normal curve. Draw and shade the curve every time you find a tail area.

This week covers: 4.5 Making Connections; 5.1 Hypothesis Testing Using Normal Distributions.
