Practice and Class Activities

These are the activities we do in class this week. Participation items are completed in class.

  • Participation: Sampling Distributions – M&M activity: each student draws 10 M&Ms, computes p̂ (blue), and adds it to a class dotplot; name the population, sample, and what each dot represents
  • Your Turn: Parameter or Statistic?
  • Participation: Sampling Distribution (Kahoot!) – review of 3.1
  • Participation: Confidence Intervals – which of 20 simulated CIs capture μ; fix three misinterpretations
  • Your Turn: Do These CIs Capture p?
