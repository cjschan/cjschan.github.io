Introduction

Now that you can find a p-value, this week is about what to do with it. In 4.3 you will compare the p-value to a significance level α and write a conclusion in context. Never say “accept H₀”. In 4.4 we look at Type I and Type II errors, multiple testing, and statistical versus practical significance. Test 2 (P.1 through 4.3) is this weekend.

This week covers: 4.3 Determining Statistical Significance; 4.4 A Closer Look at Testing.
