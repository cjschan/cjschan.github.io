Introduction

Now we describe one quantitative variable. In 2.2 you will describe the shape of a distribution and compare the mean and median, and you will read the standard deviation as a typical distance from the mean. In 2.3 we use z-scores, percentiles, the five-number summary and boxplots. Remember: the median resists outliers and skewness, but the mean is pulled toward the tail. Take-Home Quiz 1 is due Monday.

This week covers: 2.2 One Quantitative Variable: Shape and Center; 2.3 One Quantitative Variable: Percentiles.
