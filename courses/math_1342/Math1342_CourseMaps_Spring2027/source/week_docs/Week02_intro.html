Introduction

This week we learn the language of data. In 1.1 you will identify cases and variables and decide whether each variable is categorical or quantitative. In 1.2 we ask how a sample can tell us about a whole population, and how a biased sampling method can ruin that. A common mistake: zip codes, phone numbers and ID numbers are categorical, even though they are written with digits. Ask whether it makes sense to average the values.

This week covers: 1.1 The Structure of Data; 1.2 Sampling from a Population.
