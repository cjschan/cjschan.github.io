Introduction

This week we move from one variable to two. In 2.4 you will compare groups with side-by-side boxplots and describe scatterplots with direction, form, strength and the correlation r. In 2.5 (Part 1) we fit a regression line, interpret its slope and intercept, make predictions and compute residuals. Correlation is not causation. A strong r alone never shows that x causes y.

This week covers: 2.4 Two Variable Relationships; 2.5 Two Quantitative Variables: Linear Regression (Part 1).
